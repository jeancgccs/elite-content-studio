# Podcast-First Playbook

Read this when the creator's primary format is a podcast (audio-first or video
podcast). The studio system transfers — bottleneck triage, packaging-first,
designed retention, honest coaching — but the dialect and the growth physics
change. Let this file override YouTube-default advice.

## The dialect translation (use these terms, not YouTube's)

| Studio concept | Podcast dialect |
|---|---|
| Impressions | App search/browse surfaces + clip reach + guest audiences |
| CTR | Episode-title and cover clicks in search, charts, and the follower feed |
| Retention / watch time | Consumption rate (% of episode listened), drop-off point |
| Subscribers | Followers (Spotify) / Subscribers (Apple) |
| Views baseline | Downloads in the first 7 days per episode (the industry yardstick) |
| End screens / CTAs | Follow ask + one spoken CTA per episode |

## Growth physics — where new listeners actually come from

In-app podcast discovery is weak; charts are an outcome, not a lever. Rank
effort accordingly:

1. **The clip machine (highest leverage).** Every episode gets mined for 3–5
   vertical clip moments (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) — strong claims, emotional beats,
   specific stories, disagreements. Captions always; hook re-cut into frame one
   (don't start clips at the conversational ramp-up). This is the podcast
   equivalent of packaging: most future listeners meet the show as a clip.
2. **The YouTube layer.** YouTube is now the largest podcast platform. Publish
   full episodes (or chaptered video versions) there — searchable, recommendable,
   and clippable in place. A static-image "video" underperforms; even a simple
   two-camera or screen-share layout beats audio-with-artwork.
3. **Guests as distribution, not just content.** Book guests whose audience
   overlaps the target listener; make sharing effortless by delivering each
   guest a ready-to-post clip pack (their best 2–3 moments, captioned, vertical)
   within 48 hours of publish. Guesting *on* other shows converts better than
   hosting — podcast listeners follow podcasts.
4. **Episode-title search.** Titles are search queries in Apple/Spotify/YouTube:
   topic promise + specificity (+ guest name if the guest is searched). Never
   bare "Ep. 47" or inside-joke titles — label-style titles are the podcast
   version of the packaging bottleneck.
5. **Feed swaps and cross-promo** with same-size shows in adjacent niches.

## Packaging — two levels, audit both

- **Show level**: name says what it is or is memorable enough to search; cover
  art readable at thumbnail size (test at 64px — most covers fail); one-line
  positioning ("the show for [who] who want [outcome]") in the show description.
- **Episode level**: title as search query + curiosity promise; description
  front-loads the payoff (first two lines show in-app); show notes carry
  timestamps, links, and guest bio (the SEO surface); chapters mirror the
  episode's promise structure.

## Retention — designed for audio

- **Cold open**: pull the single best 20–40s moment of the episode to the very
  front, before the intro. It's the thumbnail of the audio experience.
- **Intro cap**: branded intro ≤15s after the cold open. Long intros are the
  #1 early-drop-off cause on small shows.
- **Structure per format**: interview → map the conversation beforehand (see
  interview craft below), don't wander into it; solo → script or tight
  beat-sheet with the same open-loop/payoff scheduling as a video script
  (`references/scriptwriting.md` applies nearly verbatim, humanizer pass
  included); narrative → scene-based with cliffhanger act breaks.
- **Re-hooks**: signpost what's coming ("in a few minutes, the story about X")
  at natural lulls; cut tangents in the edit ruthlessly — audio has no visual
  pattern interrupts to save a slow stretch.

## Interview craft (for interview formats)

- Pre-interview or research doc: find the 2–3 stories only this guest can tell;
  ask for *stories and specifics*, not opinions ("walk me through the day
  that..." beats "what do you think about...").
- Question order is retention architecture: strongest material early and
  seeded as open loops, not saved politely for the end.
- The host's job is follow-ups; a list of questions read in order is the
  audio version of a label-style title.

## Audit triage in podcast terms

Same four bottlenecks (`references/channel-audit.md`), podcast evidence:

- **Demand**: topics/guests nobody searches or shares — check whether episode
  concepts pass the "would a stranger click this title?" test.
- **Packaging**: episodes get follower downloads but no search/browse pickup;
  label-style titles; cover art invisible at small size.
- **Retention**: consumption rate low or cliffs early (intro too long, cold
  open missing, meandering middles) — pull per-episode drop-off in
  Spotify/Apple analytics.
- **Consistency**: irregular cadence kills feed momentum; weekly beats
  sporadic-but-longer.

Small-show reality (mirror `references/small-channel-playbook.md`): most
podcasts get under a few hundred downloads per episode — never shame the
number, benchmark against the show's own trend, and treat the first 20
episodes as reps. No invented industry benchmarks; when citing one, label
the source or call it an estimate.

## Repurposing — one episode is a content week

Episode → 3–5 vertical clips → quote graphics from the sharpest lines →
newsletter section (best insight + episode link) → transcript-based show notes
(SEO) → carousel of takeaways for IG/LinkedIn if those are creator platforms
(`references/promotion-repurposing.md` mechanics apply).

## Production floor & sound branding

- Audio quality is the trust floor: quiet room + any dedicated mic beats an
  expensive mic in echo; loudness-normalize the final mix (podcast platforms
  expect roughly -16 to -14 LUFS); record remote guests on their own track
  (Riverside/SquadCast-class tools, or at minimum a phone voice memo backup).
- Sound branding: one intro/outro theme reused every episode
  (`references/ai-toolkit.md` Suno-class guidance) — audio recognizability
  compounds; AI narration/voice rules from ai-toolkit apply unchanged.
- Transcripts for every episode: accessibility + the raw material for clips,
  notes, and search (`yt-dlp`/Whisper per `references/research-ideation.md`).
