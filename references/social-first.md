# Social-First Playbook (Instagram / TikTok as the PRIMARY platform)

Use when the creator's home base is Instagram or TikTok (or Threads/X), not
YouTube. This file reorders the workflow: the **account** is the product, the
**profile** is the packaging, and content ships in higher volume as repeatable
formats. Platform specs live in `platforms.md`; short-form script physics in
`scriptwriting.md` — this file covers what's different when social is primary.

## The follow-conversion path (the metric that matters)

On interest-graph platforms every post reaches strangers, but growth only
happens when: post stops the scroll → viewer watches/saves → visits profile →
decides to follow **in a few seconds**. Diagnose along that path:

- Reach/plays but few profile visits → content lacks a "who is this person"
  pull; add identity/POV to posts and a reason to want more.
- Profile visits but few follows → **profile problem** (see below).
- Follows but no repeat engagement → no format series or Stories layer.

Metrics dialect (use these, not YouTube's CTR/impressions): reach & plays,
3s-hold and completion rate, **saves + shares per reach** (the power signals),
profile visits per reach, follows per profile visit, comments per reach.

## Profile optimization — packaging for the account

Audit and rebuild in this order:

1. **Photo/handle**: face or bold mark legible at tiny size; handle searchable
   and consistent cross-platform.
2. **Name field ≠ handle**: put the niche keyword in the display name
   ("Ana | Fitness for Busy Moms") — IG/TikTok search indexes it.
3. **Bio formula (3 lines)**: who it's for + the outcome you deliver → proof or
   specific promise ("5-min home workouts, no equipment") → CTA to the link or
   flagship content. No vague identity bios ("dreamer ✨ coffee lover").
4. **Pinned posts (IG: 3, TikTok: up to 3)**: treat as the landing page — one
   best-performing "what I do" piece, one binge-starter for a format series,
   one conversion piece (offer/lead magnet). Refresh monthly.
5. **Highlights (IG)**: 3–6 covers in brand palette acting as a menu (Start
   Here / Results / About / FAQ) — not a story graveyard.
6. **Grid coherence (IG)**: covers for reels/carousels in a consistent template
   so the grid reads as one channel; the grid is the "about page" strangers skim.
7. **Link strategy**: one link, one job (newsletter/lead magnet beats a 12-link
   tree). The owned-audience rule from `platforms.md` applies double here —
   platform reach is rented.

## The social-first operating system

- **Format series over one-offs**: 2–3 named, repeatable formats ("3 things I
  wish I knew…", a recurring on-screen character/setup, a weekly challenge).
  Series teach the algorithm and the audience what to expect, make production
  faster, and create binge paths from pinned posts. Introduce a new format only
  after one proves out or dies (kill rule: `analytics.md`).
- **Cadence & volume**: short-form rewards volume *at a quality floor* —
  typically 3–5 reels/TikToks per week + 1–2 carousels (IG), with Stories most
  days. Set cadence at 80% of sustainable, same as the 30-day program.
- **Trend vs. evergreen mix**: trends (sounds/formats) have a days-long window —
  join fast only when the trend can carry the niche message; otherwise skip.
  Balance with searchable evergreen (IG/TikTok SEO: keywords in caption,
  on-screen text, and spoken audio — all indexed).
- **The Stories layer (IG)**: Stories are retention, not reach — daily-ish
  presence with interaction stickers (polls/questions) keeps you at the front
  of the row and warms the audience DMs. Post→Story amplification: share each
  feed post to Stories with a hook sticker.
- **Comments & DMs are the community layer**: reply fast in the first hour;
  comment-to-DM funnels (manual or keyword tools) convert engaged viewers to
  owned contacts — where platforms allow them; never spam.
- **Repurposing still applies, inverted**: the reel/TikTok is the master; a
  carousel version, a Story sequence, and a text post are the spokes
  (`promotion-repurposing.md` logic, reversed).

## Carousels as a first-class deliverable (IG's save machine)

Deliver slide-by-slide, ready to design:

```
## Carousel: [working title]
Slide 1 (the thumbnail): [big hook text ≤8 words, high contrast — apply
  packaging.md title mechanisms; a scroller sees ONLY this]
Slide 2: [re-hook: the promise or the stakes — earns the swipe]
Slides 3–[n-1]: [ONE idea per slide, ≤30 words, visual note per slide]
Slide [n]: [payoff/summary + save prompt + follow reason + CTA]
Caption: [hook line first, keyword-rich, 3–8 hashtags]
Design note: [4:5, template/palette, text safe zones]
```

Design for **saves** ("I'll need this later"): checklists, frameworks, mistakes,
before/after breakdowns. Saves + shares drive carousel distribution.

## Audit mapping (for `channel-audit.md` on social-first accounts)

Same four bottlenecks, social signals:
- **Demand**: low reach across everything → topics/formats nobody's interest
  graph wants; niche drift across posts.
- **Packaging**: reach but weak 3s-hold (reels) or slide-1 swipe-past
  (carousels), or plays without profile visits → hooks/covers/profile problem.
- **Retention**: hold but low completion/loops → pacing, payoff, length.
- **Consistency**: decent posts, no compounding → no format series, erratic
  cadence, dead Stories layer, profile that doesn't convert visits to follows.
