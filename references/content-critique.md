# Content Critique (Reviewing Existing Videos, Scripts & Thumbnails)

Use when a creator shares existing work: "review my video", "why didn't this
work", "roast my thumbnail", a script draft, or screenshots. The deliverable is
a ranked fix list they can apply to the *next* upload — not a grade.

## Critique rubric (assess in this order — same order the viewer experiences)

1. **Packaging (judge at feed size)**
   - Could a stranger state the video's promise from title + thumbnail in one
     sentence? Is there a reason to click *now*?
   - Thumbnail at 120px: readable? ≤3 elements? subject separated from background?
     emotion legible? text (if any) ≤4 words and not repeating the title?
   - Title: payload in the first ~40 chars? one idea? mechanism identifiable?
   - If they shared a thumbnail image, describe exactly what you see at small
     size before judging — critique what's rendered, not what they intended.
2. **Hook (0:00–0:30)**
   - Does second one confirm the click's promise? Count the seconds of logo/
     greeting/"welcome back" before value starts — every one is viewer loss.
   - Is a loop opened that the intro doesn't close?
3. **Structure & retention design**
   - Map the actual beats: where are the re-hooks? Longest stretch with no
     change (visual, energy, stakes)? Where does the payoff land — is the best
     material saved too late or spent too early?
   - Tangents, repeated points, and mid-video "housekeeping" are the usual bleeds.
4. **Delivery & energy**
   - Pace, vocal energy (reads ~20% flatter on camera), filler words, reading-
     vs-talking feel. Note it honestly; it improves with reps, not shame.
5. **Edit & sound**
   - Dead air, cut rhythm vs. genre norms, caption quality/safe zones, music
     under dialogue, audio levels (the #1 amateur tell).
6. **Metadata** (if shared) — against `publishing-seo.md` checklist.

## Output format

```
## Critique: [item]
**Working well**: [2–3 specific strengths — real ones, with timestamps/details.
  These are what to KEEP; struggling creators over-rotate and discard strengths.]
**Ranked fixes**:
1. [Fix] — Impact: [high/med] | Effort: [low/med/high] — [why, tied to CTR/
   retention/trust mechanism] — [concrete how, or rewritten example]
2. ...
**Rewrite samples**: [redo their actual title/hook/first lines so they can see
  the difference, not just hear about it]
**One thing to practice on the next 3 videos**: [single highest-leverage habit]
```

## Critique rules

- 3–5 ranked fixes maximum. A 20-item list feels thorough and changes nothing.
  Rank by impact ÷ effort, and lead with anything that costs minutes
  (retitle, re-thumbnail, trim the intro) before anything that costs a reshoot.
- Always rewrite, don't just describe: show the fixed title, the rewritten hook,
  the tightened first 30 seconds using their actual content.
- Calibrate to their stage (see `small-channel-playbook.md`): a 200-sub creator
  gets packaging-and-hook fundamentals, not color-grading notes.
- Honest but never contemptuous. The tone is a senior editor who wants the next
  video to win, and who says clearly when something is genuinely good.
- If asked to compare against a specific successful video, extract the
  *principles* that video uses — never suggest copying it.
